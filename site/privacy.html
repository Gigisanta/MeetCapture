Privacy, cookies and external services

Last updated: September 24, 2026

In brief: this website has no analytics, advertising pixels, embedded third-party content, or tracking scripts. Its source sets no cookies or browser storage. The macOS app transcribes locally; optional summaries use the endpoint you configure.

The MeetCapture website

The page and its fonts, icons, and product preview are served as local site assets. It does not load analytics, advertising, externally hosted fonts, video embeds, or third-party scripts. The page source does not set cookies or use local storage. No non-essential tracking runs, so there is no tracking-consent choice to save.

The deployment configuration uses Vercel for hosting. The hosting provider may process technical request data, such as IP addresses and logs, and may use its own cookies or similar technologies as described in its Privacy Notice and Cookie Policy. MeetCapture does not add Vercel analytics to this page.

The macOS app

MeetCapture captures and transcribes audio on your Mac and stores searchable meeting history locally. The first time it transcribes, it downloads its speech models (about 0.6 GB) from Hugging Face; after that it needs no network, and audio and transcripts never leave the Mac. Audio and Calendar access remain subject to the permissions managed by macOS; this notice does not change those permissions or the app's capture and consent behavior.

Summaries are optional. When the summary dispatcher runs, it sends the meeting title and date, transcript text, notes, and any configured tag-vocabulary names to the OpenAI-compatible endpoint in your local MeetCapture configuration. The default endpoint is http://127.0.0.1:8080/v1; if you change it to a remote service, that service receives the request and its own privacy terms apply. The dispatcher sends text, not the raw audio file. An optional post-summary script that you configure receives a summary JSON file that also contains your notes; that script may pass its contents to another service.

Audio retention follows the app's retention setting and the summary configuration. This page does not promise a fixed retention period for local files; review the current settings before recording.

External services

This page does not embed GitHub or MaatWork content. Following the source-code link opens GitHub; visiting MaatWork opens its website. Those services receive a request only when you follow the link and handle it under their own policies. See GitHub's Privacy Statement and MaatWork's privacy page.

Privacy requests

For a question or a request about information sent to MaatWork, use this form. It opens your configured email app; it does not submit data to this website. Review the message in your email app and send it yourself. If no email app opens, write to maatwork.comercial@gmail.com. This request is not a marketing subscription.

Send a privacy request

Include only the details needed to understand your request. Don't include passwords, API keys, meeting recordings, or other people's personal information.

MeetCapture is a MaatWork product. The MaatWork privacy page lists the available contact channel. For general information about personal-data rights in Argentina, see the AAIP guide.
