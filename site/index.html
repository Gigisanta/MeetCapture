A native app for macOS

Meeting audio, made searchable.

MeetCapture transcribes meeting audio on your Mac and keeps a searchable history close at hand. It lives only in your menu bar; click its icon to open the controls in a popover.

Made for a local workflow

Keep the details. Find them when you need them.

MeetCapture brings transcription, meeting history, and optional follow-up tools together in one native Mac app.

01

Transcribe on your Mac

After each call, NVIDIA Parakeet transcribes your microphone and the call audio separately on the Neural Engine, leaving the GPU free for a local model.

02

Search your history

Keep meeting transcripts in a searchable local history. You can also import audio and transcribe it from the app.

03

Choose what to add

Your side is labeled apart from the call, and optional diarization separates the other participants. The summary dispatcher supports OpenAI-compatible endpoints.

Audio transcription runs locally. Summary generation uses your configured endpoint, with a local route as the default.

Get started

Set up MeetCapture on your Mac.

Build and launch MeetCapture. The speech models download on first use.

Apple Silicon · macOS 14.4 or newer 

git clone https://github.com/Gigisanta/MeetCapture.git
cd MeetCapture
./install.sh

Speaker separation needs macOS 15. Read the setup details.
